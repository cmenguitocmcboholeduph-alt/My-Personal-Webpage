💗 About Me

A little bit about who I am

Hello! My name is Chairien Menguito . I am 23 years old and a proud mother of one wonderful 4-year-old son. I am currently pursuing my studies in Bachelor of Science in Information Systems. I enjoy learning new things, being creative, and continuously improving my skills. As an Information Systems student, I am interested in technology, design, and creating useful digital solutions. I love creating designs and using tools such as Draw.io to develop diagrams and organize ideas. Aside from my studies, I am also involved in our family business, which includes a quail farm and pig raising. Through this experience, I have learned the importance of responsibility, patience, hard work, and managing different tasks. I am always willing to learn, explore new ideas, and develop my skills both in technology and business. My goal is to continue growing as a student, a mother, and a future Information Systems professional.
